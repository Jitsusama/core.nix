# What Went Wrong on 15 November

On 15 November 2026 Joel looked at five pages built that day (two
proposals, the home of his local sites, a page of how his system stands
and a page of his code) and said
"the ui is bad, the ux is bad", that they looked designed by an engineer,
and that "the pages mostly have the same issues." All five were built on
the kit's `theme.css` and `base.css`, under a web-design skill of 864 to
897 lines that ranked five gates first and "the look" last. The four sites
he's proud of were built outside that skill or before it: the Ledger and
the Crossing with their own stylesheets, Slack Water with its own faces,
the Spring taking the kit's colours but not its base. Read this once per
site, so you know the engineered page on sight.

## The Template They Share

Each move below is on all five turned-down pages and on none of the loved
ones.

- **Left-aligned first screen with no composition.** The title starts at
  the column's left edge under a 20 px glyph; nothing is centred, nothing
  frames it. The loved pages centre a drawn crest, an eyebrow, the title,
  the lede and the contents on one axis.
- **The key line and the switch as chrome at the top right.** "j and k step
  from part to part, and ? lists every key" sits across from nothing, with
  a boxed switch beside it. The Crossing sets the same line small and centred
  under its contents, as part of the page.
- **A tiny mark.** A 20 to 40 px icon where the loved pages draw a 200 to
  260 px crest in the story's metaphor.
- **No eyebrow, and an upright lede.** The title is followed by a large
  upright paragraph, where the loved pages have a tracked mono eyebrow
  above the title and an italic lede below it.
- **A title broken huge over two lines with the turn on its own line**
  ("The One / Change"), and no full stop or turn where one reads ("The One
  Change" with the italic word alone).
- **"Facts as of 15 November, 19:20 Eastern." in mono** where the loved
  pages have one standing line led by a glyph, saying where the work is.
- **Mono for names and labels everywhere.** Row titles in mono on Your
  Sites, "APP" kind tags, "Asks:" labels, "Worth a look" tags on every row
  of Your System Now: labels and tags the loved pages never use.
- **Filled card rows.** The selected row is a bright filled box with
  rounded corners (Your Sites, Your System Now, Code). The loved pages part
  rows with hairlines and show the cursor by weight.
- **An app shell of two panes and a strip.** Your System Now and Code open
  on a mono strip of "All your sites · App · as of · Asks", a header bar,
  and two scrolling panes split by a rule, where Slack Water opens on a
  sentence ("38 open, 6 moving.") over its list.
- **No drawing on the first screen**, and no world: the kit's forest green
  and gold, as given, on every page.

## Where the Skill Pushed Them

- "Five things are gates, not polish ... A model reading this cold weighs
  them first", then "Then the look": the design came last, so it was what
  got skipped.
- "The page to build like is omni-ref ... One quiet line under its head
  says how to move ... The three-way switch sits in the head": followed
  literally, every page grew the same strip of chrome.
- "Fraunces for every word to read and every heading; the theme's
  Monaspace for names that are identifiers ... and labels": read as
  licence to set rows, kinds and states in mono.
- "Copy the folder whole ... load it before the site's own CSS" with
  "Override it only for a reason you can name": every page inherited one
  look, and none named a reason to leave it.
- Nothing taught a world, a crest at the size of a crest, a centred
  composition, an eyebrow, an italic lede, or the standing line: the
  moves that make the loved sites what they are.

## Tells

Look at your own first screen at 1440 and 390. Any of these means you've
built the engineered page:

- The title starts at the left edge under a small icon.
- A line of key hints or a switch sits at the top right.
- There's no drawing on the first screen.
- There's no eyebrow over the title, or the lede is upright.
- Row titles, kinds or states are in mono capitals.
- A selected row is a filled, rounded box.
- Every row carries the same tag ("Worth a look", "APP").
- The page's colours are the kit's, untouched, and nothing in it comes
  from its subject.
- Its stylesheet has no opening comment naming a world.
- Set beside the Ledger or Slack Water, it looks like a different family.
