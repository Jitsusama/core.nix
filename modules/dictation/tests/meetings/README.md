# Meetings

What three AMI meetings' speakers said in their first ten minutes, the
reference `call-transcripts score` checks its transcripts against
(`call-transcripts-hears-meetings-as-well-as-it-did`). Each line is a
start and end in seconds, the AMI speaker's letter and their words, taken
from AMI's manual transcripts under CC BY 4.0 and split at 14 s, the
longest a take runs. `meetings.nix` fetches the recordings they go with.
