The Mycelium

    Keys

    Arrows, h j k l
    On the map, walk to the nearest thing that way on the same level: the networks, or one network's cells
    Return
    Open what you're on: centre it and show it in the drawer; on a network, go in to its cells
    The two rings
    The coloured ring with a halo is where the keys are; the heavy dark ring is what's open
    -
    Step back out of what's open, or up a level to what it hangs from
    Esc
    Let go: close the drawer and clear what's open, with the map left where it is; a click on empty map does the same
    .
    Open Now in the drawer: who needs you and what holds the flow first, then how the whole network stands, what it spent today, how to read the map, and the network as a tree; again to close it. The last control over the map does the same
    a
    Open the next cell that needs you, those asking you something first, then those stuck; again for the one after
    g
    Go to the open cell: raise its window, or open one where it has none, as its drawer's Go to It does
    t
    Talk to the open cell in the Launcher, as its drawer's link does
    s
    Start new work: say it in your words, and Return hands them to the Launcher's Start New Work, from the open cell unless you untick it; nothing starts until you press Return there. Esc closes the line. The control beside Now and a cell's drawer do the same
    Shift and arrows
    Move the map
    i o
    Zoom in or out; + zooms in too. Zoom in on a network and the arrows walk its cells; zoom back out and they walk the networks
    0
    Show the whole map
    m
    Show the machines the networks run on, each network's cord running to its own; again to hide them. Opening a machine shows them too
    /
    Find a cell or network by name: what doesn't match fades back; Return opens the first match, and n and N step to the next and the one before. Esc closes the find
    The line over the map
    Where the keys are, from the network down; any step takes you back to it
    The words at the top
    How the network stands; each count opens Now, where what it counts is listed
    Tab
    The counts at the top, the look, where the keys are, the map's controls, the map, then the drawer while it's open, with the network as a tree at its end, except in a cell's, which says only where it sits
    In the tree
    j and k step, l opens a branch, h closes it or climbs, g and G go to the first and last
    ? F1
    This list, and again to close it; Esc closes it too
