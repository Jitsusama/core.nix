Asking the mycelium node.

Each cell here works on a quest, and its purpose is what that quest says it's for.

Reaching the mycelium node.

Press j and k to step through the cells, / to find one, Return to open it on the map, and ? for every key.

The cells

Whole is a purpose written, hollow is none yet; a branch was started by the cell above it or split from it, dashed where it's only claimed, a dotted arc joins a cell to one serving a need of its, and a cap rises where plans have landed steps.

The Cell Open

Keys

j k
Step to the next or the last cell; the one you're on opens beside the list
g G
Go to the first or the last cell
-, h
Step up to the cell that started the one you're on, or it split from
/
Find a cell by its name, its quest, its purpose or a document's title; Return keeps the find, Esc ends it
Return
Open the cell you're on in the map
y
Copy the command that says the cell's forage in a terminal
? F1
This list, and again to close it; Esc closes it too
